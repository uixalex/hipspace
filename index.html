How a mission takes off.

Whether it's a brand, a website or a digital product like a web or mobile app, every project moves through the same four stages, so you always know what's happening and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Discover & define

    We start with a call and a short brief to get to know your business, your goals and your audience. Then we define what makes you different and, for websites and apps, who will use them and which features matter most.

    • Kick-off call
    • Research
    • Positioning
    • Messaging
    • User research
    • Feature scope
  2. 02

    Concept & UX

    We explore creative directions through moodboards and choose the strongest one together. For websites and apps we map the user flows and wireframes, then turn them into a clickable prototype you can try out and test.

    • Moodboards
    • Creative direction
    • User flows
    • Wireframes
    • Clickable prototype
    • Usability testing
  3. 03

    Design

    The chosen direction grows into the full system: identity, website and app UI for desktop and mobile, campaign assets and a design system of reusable components, refined over two to three rounds of feedback.

    • Identity
    • UI design
    • Design system
    • Mobile & desktop
    • Social
  4. 04

    Launch & beyond

    Smaller sites we build ourselves in Framer. For larger websites and apps we hand your developers everything they need. We test, launch, and can stay on for new features, campaigns and ongoing design work.

    • Framer build
    • Dev handoff
    • Testing
    • Launch
    • Guidelines
    • Ongoing support

Questions clients ask before we start.

01

What does a project with Hipspace include?

Depends on the service. Branding includes strategy, positioning, logos, visual systems, packaging and print materials. Web design includes UI/UX, responsive design, prototypes and build. Product design covers web and mobile apps: user research, user flows, wireframes, clickable prototypes and the final UI. Social and campaign design includes visual direction and ongoing content design.

02

Can I hire you for branding and a website together?

Yes. Many projects combine branding and web design. The visual identity informs the website.

03

Can you build the website too?

For smaller projects, I can design and build websites in Framer and similar tools. For larger or more complex projects, I focus on the design, while development is handled separately by a developer chosen by the client or brought in for the project.

04

What do you need from me to get started?

A brief about your business, goals, who you're trying to reach, and timeline. Plus inspiration or references if you have them.

05

How long does a project take?

Varies. Branding typically 4–8 weeks. Web projects depend on complexity. Campaigns are ongoing. I give a clear timeline upfront.

06

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different, so the cost depends on the scope, timeline and deliverables. Once I understand what you need, I’ll put together a tailored proposal.

07

What if I don't like the first design direction?

The project includes revisions. Typically 2–3 rounds per phase to explore directions and refine. If we need additional revisions beyond that, we can discuss timeline and costs separately.

08

What if I already have a brand? Can you work on specific services?

Absolutely. I work with existing brands too. Whether you need just a website, social media content, a brand refresh, or anything in between, we can focus on exactly what you need.

09

Can you keep working with me after launch?

Yes. I offer post launch support for branding updates, new campaigns, website tweaks, or any design work you need. We can set that up as ongoing retainer or per project.

10

Do you work with businesses outside your country?

Yes, I work remotely with clients everywhere.
