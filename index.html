Questions clients ask before we start.

01

What does a project with Hipspace include?

+

Depends on the service. Branding includes strategy, positioning, logos, visual systems, packaging and print materials. Web design includes UI/UX, responsive design, prototypes and build. Social and campaign design includes visual direction and ongoing content design.

02

Can I hire you for branding and a website together?

+

Yes. Many projects combine branding and web design. The visual identity informs the website.

03

Can you build the website too?

+

For smaller projects, I can design and build websites in Framer and similar tools. For larger or more complex projects, I focus on the design, while development is handled separately by a developer chosen by the client or brought in for the project.

04

What do you need from me to get started?

+

A brief about your business, goals, who you're trying to reach, and timeline. Plus inspiration or references if you have them.

05

How long does a project take?

+

Varies. Branding typically 4–8 weeks. Web projects depend on complexity. Campaigns are ongoing. I give a clear timeline upfront.

06

How much does a project cost?

+

Every project is different, so the cost depends on the scope, timeline and deliverables. Once I understand what you need, I’ll put together a tailored proposal.

07

What if I don't like the first design direction?

+

The project includes revisions. Typically 2–3 rounds per phase to explore directions and refine. If we need additional revisions beyond that, we can discuss timeline and costs separately.

08

What if I already have a brand? Can you work on specific services?

+

Absolutely. I work with existing brands too. Whether you need just a website, social media content, a brand refresh, or anything in between, we can focus on exactly what you need.

09

Can you keep working with me after launch?

+

Yes. I offer post launch support for branding updates, new campaigns, website tweaks, or any design work you need. We can set that up as ongoing retainer or per project.

10

Do you work with businesses outside your country?

+

Yes, I work remotely with clients everywhere.
