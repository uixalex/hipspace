About the studio

Before
you ask

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different, so the cost depends on the scope, timeline and deliverables. Once I understand what you need, I'll put together a tailored proposal.

How long does it take?

Branding typically takes 4–8 weeks. Websites depend on how big they are. You get a clear timeline before we start.

Do you work with clients abroad?

Yes, I work remotely with clients everywhere.

What if I don't like the first direction?

That's what the direction stage is for. We agree on one idea before anything gets polished, and each phase includes 2–3 rounds of revisions.

What do you need from me to start?

A short brief about your business, your goals, who you're trying to reach and your timeline. References you like help too.
