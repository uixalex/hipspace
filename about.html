What we do

One studio for the whole brand.

Hipspace helps young companies look as good as their ideas. We shape the strategy behind a brand, design the identity that makes it recognisable, and bring it to life on websites, in apps and across social media.

Because it is all designed in one place, the logo, the website and the product feel like they belong to the same brand. That is what makes a small business look established, and a startup ready for its next step.

We take on a small number of projects at a time, so every brand gets proper attention from the first idea to launch day and beyond.
