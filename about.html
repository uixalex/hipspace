A small brand and digital design studio. I run it from Banja Luka and work with brands everywhere.

Hipspace exists because good ideas deserve to look as good as they are.

I shape the strategy behind a brand, design the identity that makes it recognisable, and bring it to life on websites, in apps and across social. Because it's all designed in one place, the logo, the site and the product feel like the same brand from day one.

My background is in product and UI/UX design, so I care as much about how a brand works as about how it looks. I take on a few projects at a time, and every one goes through my hands, from the first call to launch day and after.

Working together,
step by step.

What happens in each phase and what you have at the end of it.

01 Talk

A call about your business, your audience and what the project really needs. Then a short written brief, so we agree on the problem before solving it.

  • Written brief
  • Scope
  • Timeline and quote

02 Direction

I explore directions through moodboards, and for apps the user flows and wireframes, then we choose one together. One idea, agreed on, before anything gets pretty.

  • Moodboards
  • One agreed direction
  • Flows and wireframes for products

03 Design

The direction grows into the full thing: identity, website or app interface, plus the pieces your team will reuse. I make it, you react, we fix it.

  • Full design
  • 2–3 rounds of revisions
  • Design system

04 Launch

Smaller sites I build myself in Framer; for larger sites and apps your developers get everything they need. After launch I stay on for tweaks, campaigns and whatever comes next.

  • Live site or developer handoff
  • Brand guidelines
  • Support after launch

More
questions

What does a project with Hipspace include?

It depends on the service. Branding covers strategy, positioning, the logo, the visual system and print or packaging. Web design covers UX, responsive design, prototypes and the build. Product design covers web and mobile apps: research, user flows, wireframes, a clickable prototype and the final interface. Ongoing design covers social, campaigns and new pages.

Can you do the brand and the website, and build it too?

Yes, and doing both in one place keeps them feeling like the same brand. Smaller sites I build myself in Framer, so what you approve is what goes live. For larger or more complex projects I focus on the design and hand everything over to your developer, or one brought in for the project.

What if I already have a brand?

That's fine. I work with existing brands too, whether you need just a website, social content, a refresh, or anything in between.

What do you need from me to start?

A short brief about your business, your goals, who you're trying to reach and your timeline. References you like help too, but they're not required.

How long does a project take?

Branding usually takes 4–8 weeks. Websites and apps depend on their size. You get a clear timeline before we start.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different, so the cost depends on the scope, timeline and deliverables. Once I understand what you need, I'll put together a tailored proposal.

What if I don't like the first direction?

That's what the direction phase is for: we agree on one idea before anything gets polished. Each phase includes two to three rounds of revisions, and if we need more, we talk about time and cost openly.

Can you keep working with me after launch?

Yes. I offer support after launch for brand updates, new campaigns, website changes or any design work you need, either as an ongoing monthly arrangement or per project.

Do you work with clients outside Bosnia and Herzegovina?

Yes, I work remotely with clients everywhere.
